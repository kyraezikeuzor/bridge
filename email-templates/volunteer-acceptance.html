Get Started
1. Join the Discord: Meet other volunteers and stay updated on opportunities and announcements.

3. Read the Starter Guide: Get a full walkthrough of our tracks, how hours work, and what to expect.
4. Follow us on Instagram: @omeloraproject for live updates.
5. Visit our Linktree: All volunteer info in one place.
 
How to Earn Volunteer Hours
1. Stay Posted: New volunteer opportunities are shared weekly in Discord.
2. Request Volunteer Hours: Once you’ve completed an activity, submit it through the Hours Request Form, or log in to Bridge to claim activities and track your hours.
3. Start a Chapter at Your School (Optional): Bring Omelora’s workshops and programs directly to your school through supply drives and local events.
 
Have a question? Ask it in the #questions channel in Discord and we’ll get back to you. We’ve also answered common questions in our Instagram “FAQs” highlight, worth a quick check before you post.
Finally, remember to take pictures of your volunteer work! With your permission, we’d love to share them on social media to help spread the word about what we’re building.
